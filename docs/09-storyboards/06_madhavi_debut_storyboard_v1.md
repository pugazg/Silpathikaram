## Dramatic purpose

The sequence establishes mastery as the result of seven years of disciplined training, a measured stage and visible ensemble labour. Mādhavi's debut is an institutional public event. Royal recognition and the garland create economic and relational thresholds; they do not make her an object of magical seduction.
