## Dramatic purpose

The board makes Kāṉal Vari an active dramatic exchange. Kōvalaṉ and Mādhavi share a sophisticated musical language, but indirect performance becomes an interpretive contest. The sequence ends because Kōvalaṉ treats his suspicion as knowledge and leaves; it does not establish Mādhavi's infidelity.

## Continuity locks

- Both characters are credible musicians and interpreters.
- Mādhavi's answer may hold hurt, pride, warning and dramatic role simultaneously.
- No third lover or visual proof of infidelity appears.
- Kōvalaṉ's departure is unilateral, proud and self-protective.
- The shared yāḻ passes from Kōvalaṉ to Mādhavi and remains protected from sand and moisture.
- Coastal workers retain purposeful activity; the beach is not empty romantic scenery.
- The final sounding string carries into `SCN-PUK-015` and later returns as corrected memory.

## Research holds

Exact yāḻ construction and technique, sung musical reconstruction, puṉṉai pavilion materials, coastal craft forms and costume construction require specialist review. The concept boards do not reproduce or translate the original verses.
