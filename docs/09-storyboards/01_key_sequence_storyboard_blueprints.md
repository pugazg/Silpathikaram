# Key Sequence Storyboard Blueprints

These textual boards establish panel logic for the highest-risk sequences before illustration.

## SB-KEY-001 — Opening moral contract (SCN-PUK-001)

| Panel | Frame | Action / design requirement |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Extreme detail | Anklet as worked household object; no divine glow. |
| 2 | Wide triptych | River–sea Pukār, Madurai authority axis and Vañci hill/shrine materials without claiming simultaneity. |
| 3 | Medium hands | Property is stored, measured and handled. |
| 4 | Match detail | Anklet sound becomes title rhythm. |
| 5 | Wide system | Port labour begins; the world exists before the protagonists dominate it. |

## SB-KEY-006 — Hill recognition to ethical ending (SCN-VAN-001–030)

| Panel | Frame | Action / design requirement |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Vēṅkai wide | Damaged Kaṇṇaki encountered first by hill women. |

| 4 | Court listening | Royal power receives testimony. |
| 5 | Logistics montage | Councils, grain, documents, animals, boats and camps precede battle. |
| 6 | War cost | Mass organisation becomes wounded bodies. |
| 7 | Stone burden | Defeated crowns visibly bear political coercion. |
| 8 | Consecration work | Specialists transform stone at the river. |
| 9 | Shrine labour | Craftspeople and ritual experts build; royal command alone does not create it. |

| 11 | Ordinary ethics | Truth, restraint, charity and care enacted outside monumental spectacle. |

## Illustration gate

Before visual boards are drawn, key sequences require sign-off for anklet engineering, historical costume, ritual specificity, body-effect safety, fire zoning, animal ethics, weapon safety and shrine/stone reconstruction.
