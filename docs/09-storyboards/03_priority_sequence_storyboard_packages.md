# Priority Sequence Storyboard Packages

Status: `BOARD_PACKAGE_V1`

These six packages expand the earlier key-sequence blueprints into board-ready shot logic. Each package must retain Tamil-source precedence and mark choreography, architecture or iconography that remains under research.

## 1. Mādhavi's debut — `SCN-PUK-005`–`SCN-PUK-007`

### Board spine

1. feet, breath and repeated correction during training;
2. teacher-to-musician timing chain;
3. measured stage and acoustic test;
4. costume fastening as professional equipment;
5. ensemble visible before royal spectators;
6. Mādhavi enters a functioning institution;
7. performance transformation without fragmented objectifying coverage;
8. royal recognition after demonstrated mastery;
9. garland becomes honour and commodity;
10. Kōvalaṉ makes a conscious purchase.

Research hold: exact choreography, stage measurements, instrument construction and costume pattern.

## 2. Kāṉal Vari — `SCN-PUK-012`–`SCN-PUK-016`

### Board spine

1. yāḻ passes between technically competent hands;
2. Kōvalaṉ begins indirect sung speech;
3. Mādhavi listens as artist and intimate partner;
4. her answer uses the same form but changes emphasis;
5. audience receives ambiguity rather than an objective second lover;
6. Kōvalaṉ isolates one meaning;
7. unresolved note and physical withdrawal;
8. Mādhavi resumes disciplined work after abandonment;
9. flower-letter materials assembled;
10. seal, thread and messenger handoff become continuity inserts.

Research hold: melody, mode, yāḻ technique and botanical writing materials.

## 3. Pukār festival and sea route — `SCN-PUK-009`–`SCN-PUK-011`

### Board spine

1. water, labour and temporary construction before spectacle;
2. distinct commodity streets;
3. sacred institutions without later standardisation;
4. crowd routes and guards;
5. eleven-dance changeover labour;
6. instrument and costume maintenance;
7. profession-specific lamps;
8. harbour work continuing through festival night;
9. occupied beach and temporary pavilion;
10. dawn yāḻ handoff into Kāṉal Vari.

Research hold: Indra-festival ritual detail and exact dance repertoire.

## 4. Road and hunter ritual — `SCN-ROAD-007`–`SCN-ROAD-012`

### Board spine

1. green memory against seasonally altered pālai;
2. water loss and changing animal behaviour;
3. cave route offered as a legitimate sacred choice;
4. Kauṇṭi checks feet, water and daylight;
5. forest illusion remains viewpoint-ambiguous;
6. Kaṇṇaki reaches bodily limit;
7. settlement appears through food, tools, family and medicine;
8. ritual debt is named;
9. Sālini's altered state emerges through communal sound;
10. recognition of Kaṇṇaki does not make her self-aware as deity.

Research hold: Korravai/Aiyai material detail, protective formula and choreography.

## 5. Vañci expedition — `SCN-VAN-011`–`SCN-VAN-025`

### Board spine

1. testimony already present before royal decision;
2. Vēṇmāḷ's ethical proposal;
3. southern/Himalayan stone debate;
4. vow becomes administrative work;
5. councils, documents and provisioning;
6. departure ritual;
7. army movement as logistics, not only heroic spectacle;
8. alliance and river transport;
9. battle shown with cost and bounded geography;
10. captured kings and stone carry political consequence;
11. Ganges consecration;
12. warrior remembrance;
13. Māṭalan reconnects victory to dispersed grief.

Research hold: exact armour, banners, boats, campaign geography and consecration choreography.

## 6. Pattini consecration and ending — `SCN-VAN-026`–`SCN-VAN-030`

### Board spine

1. return song crosses differentiated landscapes;
2. veteran bodies interrupt pure victory;
3. prisoners released and civic remission announced;
4. stone enters craft process;
5. ritual specialists and artisans share the frame;
6. hill witnesses and women memory-bearers remain visible;
7. image installation follows—not precedes—local recognition;
8. recurring offerings establish maintenance and time;
9. rulers join a memory system they did not originate;
10. final ethical injunction returns attention from monument to conduct.

Research hold: final Pattini icon, shrine architecture, offerings and recurring festival practice.
