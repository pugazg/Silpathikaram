## Purpose

This pilot tests whether the court proof, royal collapse, three circuits and selective fire remain intelligible as one causal sequence:

`standing → test → material proof → institutional collapse → public vow → embodied circuits → bounded fire → civic consequence`

## Timed animatic plan

The panel register supplies exact in/out points and dialogue or sound cues. The intended rhythm changes across the pilot:

### Minutes 107.0–112.0 — procedural clarity

- Hold spatial geography longer than reaction shots.
- Let Kaṇṇaki's injured crossing establish the cost of access.
- Keep both anklets and their custody legible.
- Do not score the gems/pearls proposition as a supernatural reveal.

### Minutes 112.0–117.0 — object completes argument

- Cut from hand mechanics to scatter pattern and witnesses.
- The gem impact is fast, physical and repeatable.
- After the queen's confirmation, sound contracts.
- End on Kaṇṇaki, scattered gems and fallen sceptre; this is the primary interval.

### Minutes 117.0–123.0 — judgement becomes public and spatial

- Resume without a victory reset.
- Remembered women are invoked through Kaṇṇaki's speech and civic memory, not literal apparitions.
- Give each circuit its own institution, crowd and sound field.
- Maintain gait, breath and injury continuity.

### Minutes 123.0–131.0 — bounded catastrophe

- The fire-presence receives explicit limits before ignition.
- Rescue, evacuation and protected continuity must remain visible.
- Fire geography is causal and selective without a magical shield.
- The final view contains grief and civic survival, not triumph.

## Concept-image cautions

The generated sheets are blocking studies, not final history boards. Before layout lock:

- remove later-looking monumental towers or gopuram silhouettes;
- simplify crowns, jewellery and throne forms;
- replace generic monumental façades with the approved brick/timber/plaster vocabulary;
- preserve Kaṇṇaki's costume and injury state consistently;
- refine the fire-presence through source and religious consultation;
- test protected-category visibility with the full city map.
