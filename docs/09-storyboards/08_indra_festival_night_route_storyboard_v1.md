## Dramatic purpose

The sequence establishes Pukār as a civic machine intensified by festival rather than suspended by spectacle. Markets, port cargo, temporary construction, water, crowd routes, sacred plurality, performance labour and profession-specific lighting remain causally connected. Mādhavi's eleven-dance sequence is represented through working transformations, and the sea outing remains her choice.

## Continuity locks

- Festival routes preserve ordinary trade and emergency movement.
- Temporary structures are tested before decoration.
- No single later-period temple or chariot absorbs the city's plural ritual system.
- Mādhavi directs costume corrections; costume is professional equipment.
- Exact choreography of the eleven dances is not invented as textual fact.
- Her wrapped foot and bodily cost continue into the night route and Kāṉal Vari.
- Mādhavi chooses the sea outing; Kōvalaṉ does not command or pull her.
- Shop, food, guard, craft, net-repair, instrument and navigation lights remain functionally distinct.
- The shore is crowded and productive, not an empty romantic beach.
