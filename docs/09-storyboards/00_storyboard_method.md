# Storyboard Bible — Method

## Purpose

Storyboards translate the verified screenplay into spatial and visual decisions without inventing a new narrative layer.

## Evidence order

1. HWT source anchors.
2. Tamil screenplay Draft V1.
3. Scene architecture and runtime matrix.
4. Production-design packages.
5. Camera grammar and motif continuity.
6. Board-level interpretation.

## Required board fields

Each board must identify scene ID, source IDs, set package, panel number, shot scale, lens intention, camera movement, subject action, foreground/background information, sound cue, prop state, body/costume state, transition and research status.

## Panel rules

- Establish systems before isolating heroes in Pukār.
- Keep terrain, feet, water and loads causally visible on the Road.
- Preserve gates, authority, witnesses and evidence geometry in Madurai.
- Move from local witness to institution in Vañci.
- Never use a panel to replace material anklet proof with magical certainty.
- Selective fire requires mapped protected routes and categories.
- Rituals remain source-specific.
- Violence, war and catastrophe retain human cost.
- Local hill recognition precedes royal monumentalisation.

## Register

`evidence/storyboard_scene_register.csv` contains all 102 scenes, board priority, estimated panel count, production package and continuity motifs. Estimates are planning values, not locked shot counts.
