# Core Character Visual Continuity — Concept V1

Status: `CONCEPT_V1_RESEARCH_HOLD`

## Purpose

These sheets give storyboard and production teams a consistent working silhouette for Kaṇṇaki, Kōvalaṉ, Mādhavi and Kauṇṭi. They translate the verified performance dossiers and costume progression into repeatable visual states. They are not claims that any exact garment, coiffure, instrument or ascetic implement has been historically proved.

## Authority and limits

1. Original Tamil text and HWT evidence remain controlling.
2. Character action, bodily condition and object continuity are locked where the source supports them.
3. Garment construction, textile weave, jewellery technique, yāḻ form, stage costume and Jain ascetic implements remain `RESEARCH_HOLD` until specialist review.
4. No divine visual coding may precede community recognition of Kaṇṇaki.
5. Generated images are concept references, not casting likenesses or final costume plates.

### Mādhavi

States: disciplined trainee; debut preparation; public performer; intimate artist; letter writer; renunciant.

Locks: costume is professional equipment; ensemble labour remains visible; art is communication; renunciation is consequence and transformation, not cleansing of an allegedly shameful life.

### Kauṇṭi

Asset: `assets/visual-development/character-models-v1/04-kaundi-six-state-v1.jpg`

States: solitary ascetic; assessing witness; route guide; bodily-care protector; refuge broker; grieving renunciant.

Locks: every carried object has practical use; authority comes from discipline and terrain knowledge; she is never staged as omniscient.
