## Prompt summaries and interpretation limits

### Pukār

Generated as a wide early-historic Kāvēripūmpattinam development board linking river and sea, working harbour, commodity streets, merchant domestic space, a disciplined performance stage, festival labour, night lamps and occupied beach.

Avoid: fantasy-palace dominance, later monumental temple towers, generic temple-car imagery, empty waterfronts, or reducing Mādhavi's institution to ornament.

### Road

Generated as a continuous journey board moving from irrigated Kāviri country through seasonally altered pālai, a sacred cave threshold, forest, Aiyai/Korravai settlement, moonlit travel and the outer edge of Madurai. The travellers recur with accumulating fatigue and Kaṇṇaki's injured feet.

Avoid: Sahara-like desert, empty wilderness, anonymous “tribal” fantasy, or supernatural imagery presented as settled fact.

### Madurai

Generated as a functioning capital connected through Vaigai, moat, gate, markets, artisan work, pastoral refuge, a court arranged around material evidence, and fire that distinguishes protected people and spaces.

Avoid: pre-condemning the city as sinister, generic throne-room spectacle, a magical glowing anklet, invisible procedure, or indiscriminate disaster.

### Vañci

Generated as a sequence from vēṅkai-hill recognition and local testimony through royal listening, logistics, northern movement, stone transport, water consecration, craft labour and recurring shrine practice.

Avoid: royal discovery replacing local witness, generic “tribal” imagery, conquest glamour, later standardised temple architecture, or a giant pre-existing goddess icon.

## Production use

- Use the boards for discussion of spatial rhythm, labour density, palette, light and material contrast.
- Do not copy any single architectural or costume detail into a locked design without a registered source or an explicit adaptation decision.
- Subsequent boards must cite the relevant production package, scene IDs and evidence IDs.
- Human design review is required before concept art is promoted from `CONCEPT_V1_RESEARCH_HOLD`.
